# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues at a rate of 1.5 days per completed month of service. Unused annual leave
of up to 5 days can be carried forward to the next calendar year; anything beyond that
lapses on December 31st.

## Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward
or encashed.

## Applying for Leave
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance
for planned leave. Emergency leave can be applied for retroactively within 48 hours of
returning to work, with manager approval.

## Leave Balance
Employees can check their current leave balance on the HR portal dashboard, or by raising
a request with HR support.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks paid leave as per statutory requirements. Paternity leave is
10 working days, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.
