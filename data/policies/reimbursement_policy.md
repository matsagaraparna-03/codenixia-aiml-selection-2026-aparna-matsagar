# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible Expenses
Employees can claim reimbursement for: business travel (flights, trains, cabs), client
meeting expenses, internet bills for remote employees (up to INR 1,000/month), and
approved training or certification costs.

## Submission Process
All reimbursement claims must be submitted via the Finance portal within 30 days of the
expense being incurred, along with original invoices or receipts. Claims submitted after
30 days require additional manager and finance-head approval.

## Approval Timeline
Claims under INR 5,000 are typically processed within 5 working days. Claims above
INR 5,000 require manager approval before processing and may take up to 10 working days.

## Travel Advances
Employees traveling for business purposes can request a travel advance of up to 80% of
the estimated trip cost, to be settled with actual receipts within 7 days of returning.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal expenses, alcohol, entertainment unrelated to client engagement, and fines or
penalties (e.g., traffic violations) are not eligible for reimbursement.
