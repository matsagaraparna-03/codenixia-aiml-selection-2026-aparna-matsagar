# Work From Home (WFH) Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 3-month probation period are eligible to apply for
work-from-home arrangements, subject to manager approval and role suitability.

## Frequency
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week without prior approval, provided
they inform their manager and update their calendar status. WFH beyond 2 days per week
requires written approval from the reporting manager and HR.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office setup allowance of INR 10,000
for employees on a long-term WFH arrangement (more than 3 days/week).

## Expectations
Employees working from home are expected to be reachable during core business hours
(10 AM - 6 PM) and attend all scheduled meetings with video on, unless otherwise agreed.

## Full Remote Roles
Certain roles designated as "remote-first" during hiring are exempt from the standard
2-day WFH cap and follow a separate remote-work agreement signed at onboarding.
