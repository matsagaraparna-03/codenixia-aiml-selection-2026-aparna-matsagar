# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Day 1
HR will send an onboarding kit via email containing your offer letter, employee ID,
company email credentials, and a checklist of documents to bring (ID proof, address
proof, educational certificates, previous employment relieving letter if applicable).

## Day 1 Checklist
- Report to the HR desk at 9:30 AM for orientation.
- Collect your laptop and access card from the IT desk.
- Complete your bank account details and PF/tax declaration forms on the HR portal.
- Meet your reporting manager and team for a welcome session.

## First Week
New employees are enrolled in a mandatory compliance training (POSH, data security,
code of conduct) to be completed within the first 5 working days. Probation period is
3 months from the date of joining, at the end of which a performance review is conducted.

## Benefits Enrollment
Health insurance and other benefits enrollment must be completed within 15 days of
joining via the HR benefits portal. Dependents can be added to the health insurance
plan during this window or during the annual open-enrollment period.
